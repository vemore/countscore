# Play Store Image Requirements

What Google Play accepts for each image of the CountScore listing, in one place. The copy
limits (title, descriptions, release notes) are in `README.md`, "Limits"; how each image is
produced, and what is published, is `.llmwiki/StoreListing.md`.

**Last Updated**: 2026-09-24

**Nothing here is a design choice to make.** The palette is settled in
`lib/utils/app_theme.dart` (`kBrandSeedLight`, `kBrandSeedDark`, `kLeaderGold`): read the
colours there, never off a document. The icon is generated from the vector source in
`design/icon/`, and its constraints are `.llmwiki/Release.md` §Icons. The feature graphic is
generated by `scripts/generate_feature_graphic.py`; its layout, safe zone included, is
`FEATURE_GRAPHIC_TEMPLATES.md` Template 1.

## The limits

| Image | Dimensions | Format | Alpha | Size | Count | Produced by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| App icon | 512 × 512, exact | 32-bit PNG | allowed | ≤ 1 MB | 1 | `scripts/generate_icons.py` → `assets/icon_512.png` |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500, exact | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | **refused** | — | 1 | `scripts/generate_feature_graphic.py` → `assets/feature_graphic.png` |
| Phone screenshots | each side 320–3840 px, ratio ≤ 16:9 | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | **refused** | ≤ 8 MB each | 2–8 | `scripts/compose_screenshots.py` → `<locale>/screenshots/phone/` |
| Tablet screenshots (optional) | idem; 1536 × 2048 recommended | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | **refused** | ≤ 8 MB each | 1–8 | none yet |
| Promo video (optional) | a YouTube URL | — | — | — | 1 | `<locale>/video.txt` |

What each limit means here:

- **Ratio ≤ 16:9** is measured long side over short side, in either orientation: at most
  1.778. A modern phone capture is taller than that (the Pixel 9 Pro XL captures 1008 × 2244,
  ratio 2.22, with an alpha channel), so a raw `adb` capture is **never** a valid screenshot:
  it is the input of `compose_screenshots.py`, which writes 1080 × 1920 opaque RGB, exactly
  16:9. `compose_screenshots.py --check` verifies size and mode.
- **No alpha** on the feature graphic and the screenshots means an opaque 24-bit RGB file;
  a PNG saved as RGBA is refused even when every pixel is opaque.
- **The icon** must be a full square with no rounded corners and no shadow outside it: Play
  applies its own mask. No ranking or price claim ("#1", "Free") on it or on the feature
  graphic (Play Metadata policy).
- **The feature graphic** is every locale's unless `<locale>/feature_graphic.png` exists.

`play_publish.py listing --graphics` uploads the icon, the feature graphic and each locale's
composed screenshot set; it refuses a ninth screenshot and a locale without a composed set.
Nothing but Play itself checks the icon and feature graphic limits.

## Where the files are

```
store_listing/
├── assets/
│   ├── icon_512.png          generated, from design/icon/
│   └── feature_graphic.png   generated, every locale's unless it has its own
└── <locale>/
    ├── raw/                  the locale's raw captures (scripts/capture_screenshots.sh <locale>)
    ├── screenshot_captions.txt
    └── screenshots/phone/    composed, what Play gets
```
