Keep track of scores for all your favorite games with CountScore – a simple, privacy-focused score tracking app.

KEY FEATURES

Multiple Game Types
CountScore comes with pre-configured support for popular games like Uno and Scrabble. You can also create custom game types with personalized names, colors, and icons to match any game you play.

Flexible Scoring
Whether your game rewards the highest or lowest score, CountScore adapts. Easily enter and adjust scores as you play.

Player Management
Add unlimited players and track their individual statistics across multiple games and sessions.

Complete Game History
Review the full score history for every game. Track progress over time and settle those "who won last time?" debates.

Works Offline
All data is stored locally on your device. No internet connection required to track a single score – perfect for camping trips, flights, or anywhere you play.

ZapZap Analysis (optional)
Finished a game of ZapZap? Ask for a written commentary on how it went. This is the one feature that uses the internet: when you tap the button, that game's scores are sent to our server and on to an AI model to write the analysis. It only ever runs when you ask for it.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create or select a game type
2. Add your players
3. Track scores as you play
4. View history and statistics anytime

PERFECT FOR

Family game nights, card tournaments, board game sessions, sports scorekeeping, classroom activities, and party games. If it needs a score, CountScore handles it.

PRIVACY

No accounts, no cloud sync, no ads and no tracking. Your games stay on your device, with one exception you control: tapping "generate analysis" sends that game's data to our server and on to an AI provider to write the commentary, and nothing else is ever sent. Full details in our privacy policy. CountScore is open-source (MIT License), so you can verify all of this yourself.

COMPLETELY FREE

No ads, no in-app purchases, no hidden costs. CountScore is free forever.
