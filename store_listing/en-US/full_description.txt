Keep track of scores for all your favorite games with CountScore – a simple, privacy-focused score tracking app.

KEY FEATURES

Multiple Game Types
CountScore comes ready for popular games such as ZapZap, Uno, Scrabble, Skyjo, Belote and Tarot. You can also create your own game types with the name, color and icon you like.

Flexible Scoring
Whether your game rewards the highest or lowest score, CountScore adapts. Enter and adjust scores round by round, with running totals and a live ranking.

Players and Statistics
Players are shared across games, so their statistics follow them from one game to the next.

Complete Game History
Review the full score history of every game and settle those "who won last time?" debates.

Works Offline
Your games are stored on your device. No internet connection is needed to keep score – perfect for camping trips, flights, or anywhere you play.

Group Sharing (optional)
Keep the same games on several phones – the whole family's, say. Create a group or join one with an invite code, then share games: scores entered on one device appear on the others, and changes made offline catch up when you reconnect. Games you don't share stay on your device.

ZapZap Analysis (optional)
Finished a game of ZapZap? Ask for a written commentary on how it went. It only runs when you tap the button: that game's scores, player names and round comments are sent to your server, which asks an AI model to write the analysis. If a commentary is wrong or offensive, "Report this commentary" lets you tell us by email.

YOUR OWN SERVER

Group sharing and the ZapZap analysis need a server, and CountScore ships without one. They stay switched off until you enter the address of a server you run – the server software is open source – in Settings → Server. Without it, the app makes no network request at all.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create or select a game type
2. Add your players
3. Track scores as you play
4. View history and statistics anytime

PERFECT FOR

Family game nights, card tournaments, board game sessions, classroom activities and party games. If it needs a score, CountScore handles it.

PRIVACY

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your games stay on your device unless you choose otherwise: a game you share with a group is stored on the server you configured and sent to the group's devices, and an analysis you request is sent to that server and on to the AI provider it uses. Joining a group gives your device an access token – never an account. Full details in our privacy policy. CountScore is open source (MIT License), so you can verify all of this yourself.

COMPLETELY FREE

No ads, no in-app purchases, no hidden costs.
