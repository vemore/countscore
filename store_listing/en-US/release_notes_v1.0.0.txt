🎉 CountScore 1.0.0 - Initial Release

Welcome to CountScore! We're excited to bring you simple, powerful score tracking for all your games and activities.

✨ Features in this release:

📊 SCORE TRACKING
• Track scores for multiple game types
• Support for lowest-wins and highest-wins scoring
• Easy score entry and management

🎮 GAME TYPES
• Pre-configured games (Uno, Scrabble, and more)
• Create custom game types
• Personalized colors and icons

👥 PLAYER MANAGEMENT
• Add and manage unlimited players
• Track players across different games
• Easy player organization

📈 GAME HISTORY
• Full score history for all games
• Review past game results
• Track progress over time

🎨 CUSTOMIZATION
• Custom colors for each game type
• Material Design 3 interface
• Clean, intuitive design

🔒 PRIVACY FOCUSED
• All data stored locally on your device
• No data collection or tracking
• No ads or third-party services
• Open-source and auditable

📴 WORKS OFFLINE
• No internet connection required
• Perfect for any location
• Lightweight and fast
